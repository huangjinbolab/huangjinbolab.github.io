title: "Principal Investigator"
---

Dr. Jinbo Huang is a Principal Investigator at the Hangzhou Institute of Medicine, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), and a recipient of the National Overseas Young Talent Program. He completed his integrated bachelor's-master's training at Huazhong Agricultural University in 2019. During 2019-2025, he pursued Ph.D. and postdoctoral training at ETH Zurich under the supervision of Prof. Martin Fussenegger, where he developed the first electrogenetic system enabling direct-current control of mammalian gene expression, along with additional gene switches responsive to ultrasound, chemical small molecules, ultrashort peptides, and the safe drug aspirin. Cell therapies based on these systems were successfully applied to metabolic disease models, including diabetes. Since December 2025, he has joined the Hangzhou Institute of Medicine, CAS, to establish the "Intelligent Cell Engineering and Precision Medicine" Laboratory. In recent years, as independent first author, he has published eight papers in leading journals including Nature Metabolism, Nature Communications, Advanced Science (two papers), Trends in Biotechnology, and Nucleic Acids Research, along with one co-first-author paper and several co-author papers.
